# Xenon's Tinkering Materials
A thingpack for Tinkers' Construct that adds a collection of new materials. Requires Json Things and Tinkers' Construct 3.12.0+

https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/xenons-tinkering-materials


## Material List
* **Aurucelium:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/aurucelium.png">
	- Thermophile: Grants increased damage and protection in hot biomes.
	
* **Bluehat:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/bluehat_ingot.png">
	- Anti-Hat (Melee, Ranged): Grants increased damage if you or the target is wearing a hat.
	- Platformer (Armor): Lets you move faster, fall further without getting hurt, and boots let you double jump.
	
* **Brimsteel:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/brimsteel_ingot.gif">
	- Thermodynamic (Harvest, Ranged): Grants increased mining speed, drawspeed and accuracy while on fire.
	- Firewall (Armor): Grants increased protection while burning but you take more damage from fire.
	
* **Deepbrass:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/deepbrass_ingot.png">
	- Subterranean: Grants increased damage and reinforces armor the further underground you are.
	
* **Ferroshroom:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/ferroshroom.png">
	- Deciduous: Tool shares its durability with other tools when used.
	
* **Firelight Silk:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/firelight_silk.png">
	- Autosmelt (Harvest): Automatically smelts mob and block drops.
	- Tempered Protection (Armor): Protects from attackers who are burning.
	
* **Fungal:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/mushroom_medley.png">
	- Earth Eater (Harvest): Mining dirt protects the tool from damage.
	- Sporeshot (Ranged): Poisons targets.
	- Sporeburst (Armor): Poisons attackers.
	
* **Grimslime:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/grimslime_ingot.png">
	- Overreap (Melee): Tool consumes common mob drops (flesh, bones, slime) to refill overslime.
	- Overdraw (Ranged): Increased draw speed based on remaining overslime.
	- Overward (Armor): Converts some of the magic damage you take into overslime.
	
* **Hardlight:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/hardlight_sheet.gif">
	- Oscillating: Deals increased damage against armored targets and boosts mining speed against blocks with high hardness (stone, obsidian, etc.)
	
* **Jewelslime:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/jewelslime_gem.png">
	- Overheart: Boosts max health based on overslime capacity.
	
* **Midday Bronze:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/midday_bronze_ingot.png">
	- Diurnal (Melee): Grants increased damage if you've slept recently.
	- Daybreak (Ranged, Armor): Grants increased draw speed and knockback resistance in full sunlight.
	
* **Midnight Bronze:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/midnight_bronze_ingot.png">
	- Insomniac (Melee): Grants increased damage the longer you go without sleeping.
	- Nightstalker (Ranged, Armor): Grants increased damage and protection in the darkness.
	
* **Neutralite:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/neutralite_ingot.png">
	- Indifference: Grants increased damage, accuracy and protection in neutral temperatures.
	
* **Platinum:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/platinum_ingot.png">
	- Lustrous (Harvest): Grants bonus nuggets of material when mining ores.
	- Olympic (Ranged): Slain mobs have a chance of dropping metal nuggets based on how far away they are when killed.
	- Dispel (Armor): Grants increased protection against undead mobs and reduces the duration of negative potion effects.
	
* **Platinum Gold:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/platinum_gold_ingot.png">
	- Hardforged: Makes the tool harder to repair, but reinforces it when durability is low.
	
* **Pykrete:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/pykrete_bat.png">
	- Frostborn (Melee, Ranged, Armor): Grants increased damage in cold biomes.
	- Frigid (Ammo): Deals increased damage against fire immune mobs.
	
* **Soggy Paper:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/soggy_paper.png">
	- Flimsy: Grants two upgrade slots at the cost of your tool's stats.
	
* **Twilight Bronze:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/twilight_bronze_ingot.png">
	- Crepuscular: Grants increased damage in the light, boosts speed in the dark.
	
* **Vermillium:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/vermillium_ingot.png">
	- Bulwark (Melee): Grants increased damage at high health.
	- Hearty Protection (Armor): Grants increased protection at high health.
	
* **Viridium:** <img width=64 src="https://github.com/Xenon372/Image-Repository/blob/main/Xenon's%20Tinkering%20Materials/viridium_ingot.png">
	- Experienced: Grants increased experience.
